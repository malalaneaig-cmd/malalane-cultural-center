## Posting mechanics reminder
- Use **native upload** (photo/video attached directly to the post), not a pasted link — LinkedIn's algorithm favors native media and it renders better in-feed.
- Keep captions to 3–5 short paragraphs; the first 1–2 lines matter most since LinkedIn truncates with "see more."
- 3–4 hashtags per post is enough; more dilutes reach.
- Since the Page has 0 followers today, consider having a few personal profiles (e.g. Aurelio's) re-share Post 1 to seed initial visibility.
